Midday Motorist

This is Midday Motorist, a game I had customized for a school project based on a 2d shooter platform game basis and I added my own twist to it. As the game is made to help bring awareness to drivers to drive slower and keep their eyes on the road. There are multiple levels and the game is made to be a fun and interactive way to learn about the dangers of distracted driving.

Arctic Blast

This is Arctic Blast, a game I had customized for a school project based on a roll-a-ball platform game and I added my own twist to it. As the game is made to help bring awareness to polar bears and their loss of habitat and the poaching of polar bears by illegal hunting.
